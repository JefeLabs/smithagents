# Crew Notes

What the crew can do:

- **Talk it through live** — join a voice meeting, listen, and respond in real time, each agent with its own voice and meeting etiquette.
- **Take work away from the table** — delegate tasks to the swarm, where each agent works on its own branch and commits its results.
- **Cover specialist beats** — every crew member owns a domain (integration boundaries, pages, infra, etc.) and vets, drafts, and decides within it.
